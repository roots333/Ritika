Our Story

From Rajasthan's fields to your table.

Roots to Eat is being built from a simple belief: the future of Indian food can be modern without forgetting where it comes from.

We are starting in Rajasthan, with a deep connection to Indian grains and a pan-India ambition. Our goal is to make familiar, nourishing staples feel trustworthy, contemporary and accessible.
